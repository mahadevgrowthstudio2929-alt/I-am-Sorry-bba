A Letter I Couldn't Say Out Loud

❤️
Dear Bba, ❤️

I know you're angry with me, and honestly, I understand why.

I know I made a mistake. I know my actions hurt you, and I'm genuinely sorry for that.

I'm not writing this to make excuses or to ask you to forget everything immediately.

I just want you to know that I realise where I went wrong.

You mean so much more to me than I sometimes know how to express.

And maybe that's why it hurts me even more knowing that I became the reason behind your sadness.

Bba, I'm not promising that I'll suddenly become perfect.

But I can promise that I'll learn from this. I'll listen better. I'll understand better. And I'll try harder to make sure that my mistakes don't keep hurting the person I love.

I'm really, really sorry. ❤️

I don't expect you to forgive me just because I made this little website.

Take your time.

Be angry if you need to.

But whenever you're ready...

I hope you let me make things right.

I love you, Bba. ❤️

— Souvick
